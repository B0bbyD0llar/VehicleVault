We are proud to announce **VehicleVault v3.5.0**! This milestone release introduces complete **Vehicle Ownership Transfer** (seamlessly passing a digital vehicle passport to another user), **German (de) Localization**, streamlined **1-Line Quick Installers** for Windows, macOS, and Linux, and major fixes for **Maintenance Notifications & Interval Baselines**.

---

## 🌟 What's New

### 🔄 1. Complete Vehicle Ownership Transfer
- **Digital Passport Handoff:** Transfer full ownership of any vehicle to another registered user on the server.
- **Preserved History:** All associated maintenance records, invoices, fuel fill-ups, tire sets, trips, documents, and odometer logs remain attached to the vehicle and move with it to the new owner.
- **Buyer Auto-Provisioning:** If the buyer does not yet have an account, VehicleVault provisions a new account and returns a 1-click onboarding invitation link (`/login?invite=...`).
- **Flexible Access Retention:** The previous owner can optionally choose to remain as a co-driver editor or surrender access entirely upon transfer.

### 🇩🇪 2. German (de) Language Support (PR #7)
- **Full UI Localization:** Complete German translation across all dashboards, settings, glovebox documents, maintenance tracking, and fuel logging.
- **Automatic Language Detection:** Seamlessly detects German browser environments (`de`, `de-DE`, `de-AT`, `de-CH`) on initial visit.
- **Localized Date & Weekday Picker:** Full German localization for calendar pickers (`Januar`, `Februar`, `Mo`, `Di`, `Mi`...).
- **Contributed by:** `@B0bbyD0llar` in PR #7.

### ⚡ 3. One-Line Installers for Linux, macOS & Windows (PR #6)
- **Zero-Friction Deployment:**
  - **Linux / macOS:** `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TeeCodeDev/VehicleVault/main/scripts/install.sh | bash`
  - **Windows (PowerShell):** `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TeeCodeDev/VehicleVault/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex`
- **Automated Configuration:** Generates a secure directory (`~/vehiclevault` or `%USERPROFILE%\VehicleVault`), creates a cryptographically random `.env` (`JWT_SECRET`, `JWT_REFRESH_SECRET`), generates `docker-compose.yml`, pulls the multi-architecture image, and verifies the `/healthz` endpoint.
- **Contributed by:** `@9M2PJU` in PR #6.

### 🔧 4. Maintenance Intervals & Notification Anti-Spam Fixes
- **Unified Maintenance Evaluation:** Notifications now use the exact same calculation engine as the web dashboard (`MaintenanceService`), preventing premature or erroneous alerts.
- **Smart Date Baselines:** Newly added vehicles baseline their maintenance intervals to the registration date rather than ancient historical purchase dates, preventing intervals from immediately being marked as overdue.
- **Motorcycle Preset Filtering:** Motorbike-specific presets (such as *Chain & Sprockets*) are no longer automatically added to passenger cars and electric vehicles.
- **14-Day Anti-Spam Cooldown:** Overdue maintenance alerts now enforce a 14-day notification cooldown, completely eliminating annoying daily notification spam.
